Semester V - Subjects & Units

1. Computer Networks

  • Unit I – Introduction to Networks: OSI & TCP/IP Models, Network Topologies, Transmission Media
  • Unit II – Data Link Layer: Error Detection, Flow Control, Switching
  • Unit III – Network Layer: IP Addressing, Routing Algorithms, Subnetting
  • Unit IV – Transport Layer: TCP & UDP, Congestion Control
  • Unit V – Application Layer: DNS, Email, HTTP, Network Security Basics

2. Web Programming

  • Unit I – HTML5, CSS3, Forms, Responsive Design
  • Unit II – JavaScript, DOM, Events, ES6
  • Unit III – Server-Side Scripting: PHP/Python, Cookies, Sessions
  • Unit IV – Databases: SQL, CRUD Operations, Web Integration
  • Unit V – Node.js, React Basics, REST APIs

3. Algorithms for Intelligent Systems

  • Unit I – Introduction to Intelligent Systems, Search Algorithms
  • Unit II – Heuristic Algorithms: A*, Genetic Algorithms
  • Unit III – Swarm Intelligence: Particle Swarm, Ant Colony
  • Unit IV – Machine Learning Algorithms: Decision Trees, SVMs
  • Unit V – Applications: Robotics, NLP, Intelligent Agents

4. Augmented Reality

  • Unit I – AR Basics, AR vs VR, Applications
  • Unit II – AR Hardware: Sensors, HMDs, Smart Glasses
  • Unit III – AR Software: ARCore, ARKit, Vuforia, Tracking
  • Unit IV – AR Content: 3D Models, Unity, AR Interfaces
  • Unit V – AR in Education, Medicine, Industry

5. Sensors and Transducers

  • Unit I – Measurement Systems, Sensor Characteristics
  • Unit II – Mechanical Sensors: Displacement, Velocity, Pressure
  • Unit III – Thermal & Optical Sensors: Temperature, IR, Light Detectors
  • Unit IV – Smart Sensors: MEMS, IoT Integration
  • Unit V – Applications in Automation, Robotics, Healthcare

6. Disaster Management

  • Unit I – Introduction: Types of Disasters, Hazards & Risks
  • Unit II – Disaster Preparedness: Forecasting, Risk Assessment
  • Unit III – Mitigation Measures: Structural & Non-Structural
  • Unit IV – Response: Rescue, Relief, Rehabilitation
  • Unit V – Case Studies: Floods, Earthquakes, AI in Disaster Mgmt
